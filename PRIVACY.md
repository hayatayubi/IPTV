# Lumen privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Lumen is a video player for Google TV. It does not collect, share or sell any personal data.

- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** Lumen has no servers of its own and sends nothing about you or your
  viewing to anyone.
- **Your playlists stay on your TV.** Playlist links, Xtream logins, favourites, watch progress and settings are
  stored only on the device. They are not backed up to the cloud (Android backup is turned off for Lumen).
- **Who Lumen talks to:**
  - The playlist, guide and stream addresses **you** enter, to load channels, programme guides, films and series.
    Those providers see your TV's IP address, as with any streaming app, and their own privacy policies apply.
  - Channel logo and poster addresses supplied by those playlists.
  - GitHub (`api.github.com` and `github.com`), to check for and download Lumen updates. GitHub sees your IP address
    and that a Lumen update was checked or downloaded; see
    [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Removing your data:** delete a playlist in Lumen, or uninstall Lumen, and everything it stored is removed.

Lumen provides no content of its own. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/lumen-tv/Lumen-TV/issues.
